WASD move & drive · E enter / exit car · Mouse aim, Click shoot · Space handbrake · M mute · P pause

An original game. No real city, no real people, no borrowed assets.

HOW TO PLAY

  • W A S D — walk, or drive when you are in a car
  • E — steal / enter the nearest car, or get out
  • Mouse + Click — aim and shoot (on foot)
  • Space — handbrake while driving
  • Shift — sprint on foot
  • M — mute sound   P — pause

Crimes raise your wanted level. Lose the police by breaking line of sight, or lay low until the heat fades. Finish missions at the yellow markers to earn cash and unlock the next job. Your progress saves automatically.
